# کتاب کار حرکت و بازی — پایه اول — درس‌های ۶ تا ۱۲ (inventory)

Source: book.pdf, read as page images. **PDF page number = printed page number (offset 0).**
Blank filler pages (yellow bubbles, no content): 44, 50, 56, 62, 68, 74. Page 80 = back cover (feedback address only).

Structure of every lesson 7–12 is identical (lesson 6 differs, see below):
1. cover page (number + title)
2. **«حرکت و خلاقیت»** — "به قصه‌ی تاپ‌تاپو/تاپ‌تاپک گوش کن و به پرسش‌ها جواب بده" — story + 4 bullet tasks on a wooden sign. The last task is always: «به کمک بزرگ‌ترت واژه‌ی «X» را در داستان پیدا کن و انگشتت را زیر آن بگذار» (find the key word with a grown-up, put your finger under it).
3. **«برویم بازی!»** — "به شکل بازی‌ها نگاه کن. یک روز در میان یکی (یا سه‌تا) از آن‌ها را انجام بده" (every other day do one/three) + a 3-row calendar (روز اول / دوم / سوم): circle the game you did and pick a face (happy / neutral / sad) for how it felt.
4. **«بازی خانوادگی»** — "همراه با خانواده بازی کن." — one illustrated family game, almost always seated in a circle with music («با پخش آهنگ بازی»).
5. **«بازی کن و ستاره بگیر!»** — weekly page; 7 ants each holding a star; colour one star each time you beat your previous result ("هر بار که بیشتر/دورتر … یکی از ستاره‌ها را رنگ کن").
6. Leopard mascot (یوزپلنگ, "Yuzi") speech bubbles «من …» at page bottoms.

Camera verdicts assume a phone on the floor/shelf, child facing it, whole body in frame, pose detection only (no ball/object detection).
**yes** = reliably countable · **partly** = body motion countable but the real success criterion (ball caught, target hit, step on a square) is not · **no** = camera cannot judge; grown-up must confirm.

## درس ۶ — زندگی فعّال (Active life) — pp. 39–44 — خان ۳ (with lesson 5)

### Story (p40) — «حرکت و خلاقیت»: قصه‌ی پیاده‌روی
- Characters: تاپ‌تاپک، تاپ‌تاپو، مامان، بابا. Key word: **پیاده‌روی** (walking).
- Summary: The family wanted to drive to the bazaar, but Dad's car had a flat tyre. Taptapoo was upset; Mum and Dad said "never mind, we'll walk." Taptapak: "I'll get tired." Mum: "We've been sitting at home all morning; in the car we'd sit too — walking is very good." On the way they saw many people walking or cycling; in the park people were exercising. Taptapak: "That was fun! I wish Dad's car always had a flat so we could walk." Everyone laughed and reached the bazaar.
- Tasks (verbal only, no body task):
  - بگو تاپ‌تاپک و تاپ‌تاپو با مامان و بابا چطوری به بازار رفتند؟
  - تاپ‌تاپک و تاپ‌تاپو توی راه چه کسانی را دیدند؟
  - تاپ‌تاپک و تاپ‌تاپو توی پارک چه کسانی را دیدند؟
  - چرا به تاپ‌تاپک و تاپ‌تاپو خوش گذشت؟
  - به کمک بزرگ‌ترت واژه‌ی «پیاده‌روی» را در داستان پیدا کن…
- Side pictures: a man jogging, a boy cycling with helmet, a grandmother doing arm stretches.

### Family game (p42) — توافق‌های سبز و قرمز (green/red agreements), name not given
- Rules (verbatim gist): The family first agree some health, nutrition and safety rules for playing at home (e.g. don't play when someone is asleep, don't play in the kitchen, don't play on the stairs…). All sit in a circle on the floor. In front of each person: two small balls, **green** (correct behaviour) and **red** (wrong behaviour). One person is the leader («بازی‌گردان») and calls movement commands (clap, stamp feet, shake body…), everyone does them. Between commands the leader randomly states one of the agreed rules — correctly or wrongly. Whoever is fastest to lift the right-coloured ball becomes the next leader; whoever lifts the wrong colour must do a show-move or a funny trick («حرکت نمایشی یا شیرین‌کاری»).
- Players: whole family (picture: 6 — grandmother, mum, dad, 2 children, toddler).
- Equipment: 2 small balls per person (1 green, 1 red) — any green/red token works.
- Camera: no (group, reaction, colour judgement). Ideal as a **touch/phone-held quiz** with movement commands in between.

### Star page (p43) — behaviour cards
"به رفتارهای درست در شکل نگاه کن. در طول هفته، هر بار که یکی از آن‌ها را انجام دادی، یک ستاره را رنگ کن."
Six cards: (1) boy vacuuming/helping at home; (2) sick boy resting in bed — grandmother with soup, thought bubble of playing crossed out (rest when ill, don't play); (3) girl doing tree pose inside a dashed circle in an empty room (play in a safe, clear space); (4) girls taking the stairs instead of the escalator in a mall; (5) three boys on a bench eating apple/sandwich, chips crossed out (healthy snack); (6) family walking together.
Tracked: each time a card's behaviour is done → colour 1 of 7 stars. Camera: no — grown-up confirms (tree pose could be a camera mini-game).

### Behaviour messages
- p42: «من نوبت بازی را رعایت می‌کنم.» (I take turns.)
- From star cards (not «من» sentences in the book, but natural ones): help at home; rest when sick; play in a safe space; take the stairs; eat healthy snacks; walk with family.

### Equipment
pencil (maze), green + red small balls/tokens per person.

### New poses
1. Child walking with family (likely exists).
2. Tree pose: one leg, other foot on inner leg, palms together overhead (may exist from earlier lessons).
3. Child vacuuming / tidying (illustration only, no game).

## درس ۷ — بدن نرم (Soft/flexible body) — pp. 45–50 — خان ۴ (with lesson 8)

### Story (p46) — قصه‌ی ماهی پولکی (the scaly fish)
- Characters: تاپ‌تاپو، مامان (tells the story), ماهی پولکی (in Taptapoo's imagination, drawn as Taptapoo with a fish tail). Key word: **نرم** (soft/supple).
- Summary: Mum tells Taptapoo the story of the scaly fish who wanted to reach the sea; it gently bent and straightened its body and swam *softly* down the river until it reached the sea. Taptapoo closed his eyes and wished he could swim softly like the fish. Then he gently bent and straightened his own body and, in his imagination, swam softly like the fish to the sea.
- Body movements pictured (3 side frames): standing, hands on hips → one arm up over the head, bending sideways (side bend) → side bend to the other side.
- Tasks:
  - ماهی پولکی دوست داشت کجا برود؟
  - تاپ‌تاپو وقتی چشم‌هایش را بست به چه چیز فکر می‌کرد؟
  - **مانند تاپ‌تاپو هر حرکت را انجام بده و تا هشت بار تکرار کن.** (do each movement like Taptapoo, repeat up to 8 times)
  - find the word «نرم».
- Camera: **yes** — lateral side bend is in the frontal plane (shoulder tilt + raised wrist), easy to count L/R.

## درس ۸ — هماهنگی (Coordination / rhythm) — pp. 51–56 — خان ۴

### Story (p52) — قطار مدادی (the pencil train)
- Characters: تاپ‌تاپک، مامان. Key word: **هماهنگ** (in rhythm/together).
- Summary: Taptapak had lined up her pencils one behind another. Mum: "What have you made?" — "A train." Mum: "A train can't be silent! Let's make a sound for your train together." Mum patted her legs with her palms, one hand after the other, and said in rhythm: "هوهو!" Then patted her chest, one hand after the other, and said "چی‌چی!" Taptapak and Mum repeated the movements in rhythm, fast: "هوهو، چی‌چی، هوهو، چی‌چی!" Taptapak laughed at the pencil train; now its sound filled the room.
- Pictured: (1) child **sitting on a chair** patting thighs with alternate hands; (2) patting chest with alternate hands.
- Tasks:
  - تاپ‌تاپک با چه چیزی قطار درست کرد؟
  - مامان تاپ‌تاپک صدای قطار را چطور درست کرد؟
  - **مثل تاپ‌تاپک حرکات را چند بار پشت‌سرهم انجام بده و صدای قطار بساز.** (do the moves several times in a row and make the train sound)
  - find the word «هماهنگ».
- Camera: **yes** — wrists alternating at thigh level vs chest level, seated facing camera. Microphone could also hear «هوهو / چی‌چی».

### Family game (p54) — بازی لیوان‌ها (cup rhythm), name not given
- Rules: Family sit side by side in a circle; a plastic cup in front of each. With music, in rhythm:
  1. سه بار دست زدن و شمارش ۱،۲،۳ (clap ×3, count 1-2-3)
  2. گذاشتن دست راست روی لیوان و شمارش ۱ (right hand on cup, "1")
  3. گذاشتن لیوان مقابل هم‌بازی سمت راست خود و شمارش ۲ (place cup in front of the player on your right, "2")
  4. سه بار دست زدن و شمارش ۱،۲،۳ — repeat.
- Players: family (picture 7). Equipment: one plastic cup each, music.
- Camera: no (group on floor; cup passing). Claps alone could be counted for one player.

### Star page (p55) — the plus-shaped grid (خانه‌ها)
- "هربار که پشت سر هم در همه‌ی خانه‌ها حرکت کردی، یکی از ستاره‌ها را رنگ کن."
- Grid: a plus/cross of 5 squares drawn/taped on the floor: centre (red footprints) and arms numbered ۱ (front), ۲ (back), ۳ and ۴ (sides).
- Rules (verbatim):
  - در خانه‌ی وسط ۴ بار پا بکوب، به ترتیب شماره به یکی از خانه‌ها برو. (stamp 4× in the centre, go to the squares in number order)
  - در هر خانه دو بار پا بکوب و بشمار ۱،۲. (stamp 2× in each square, counting 1,2)
  - به خانه‌ی وسط برگرد، دوبار پا بکوب و بشمار ۳،۴. (back to centre, stamp 2×, counting 3,4)
  - به خانه‌ی بعدی برو و حرکات را تکرار کن. (next square, repeat)
- Tracked: each complete uninterrupted circuit of all squares → 1 star.
- Equipment: tape/chalk to mark 5 squares.
- Camera: partly — side squares (۳/۴) = lateral shift + stamps, countable; front/back squares (۱/۲) = depth movement, only weakly visible (body gets bigger/smaller). Could be adapted to a left/right-only version, or the grid can be drawn on screen.

### Behaviour messages
- p54 «من هم‌بازی‌هایم را تشویق می‌کنم.» (I cheer on my playmates.)
- (no Yuzi on pp. 52, 53, 55)

### Equipment
chair, wall, plastic cups, music player, floor tape/chalk.

### New poses
1. Sitting on chair, patting thigh — alternate hands (2 frames).
2. Sitting on chair, patting chest — alternate hands (2 frames).
3. Standing foot stamp (2 frames: knee up / foot down) — may exist.
4. Standing clap (2 frames) — likely exists.
5. Both palms pushed forward / on wall.
6. One palm pushed forward (right; mirror for left) (2 frames).
7. Sitting cross-legged, sliding a cup to the right (family).
8. Stepping sideways into a square and stamping (can reuse stamp + side step).

### Family game (p60) — سیب‌زمینی داغ با سبد (hot potato + basket), name not given
- Rules: Family sit in a circle, spaced apart; a basket in the middle. - Players: family (picture 6). Equipment: small beach ball, basket, music.
- Camera: no.

## درس ۱۰ — کار با پا (Footwork) — pp. 63–68 — خان ۵

### Story (p64) — توپ توی خواب (the ball in the dream)
- Characters: تاپ‌تاپک، مامان. Key word: **توپ** (ball).
- Summary: Taptapak was asleep; Mum called her. She woke up upset: "Why did you wake me? I was playing with a ball in my dream." Mum: "You can play with a ball awake too. Get up, get ready, bring your ball and play." They both laughed. Taptapak brought her ball and played: «یک‌بار با این پا، یک‌بار با اون پا، تالاپ تلوپ بازی با توپ.»
- Pictured (3 frames + big one): standing, one foot (sole) placed on top of a ball; switching feet — alternate toe taps on the ball.
- Tasks:
  - تاپ‌تاپک در خواب چه می‌دید؟
  - مامان تاپ‌تاپک به او چه گفت؟
  - **مثل تاپ‌تاپک با توپ بازی کن و حرکات را انجام بده.** (play with a ball like her)
  - find the word «توپ».
- Camera: partly — alternating foot lifts are countable (like marching); ball contact is not. Could be done with a "virtual ball" on screen.

### Family game (p78) — تاپ‌تاپ خمیر
- Rules (book): Family sit in a circle. One kneels in the middle, bends forward bringing the forehead close to the floor and covers the eyes with both hands. The others place their hands on his back and, with very gentle rhythmic pats, chant: **«تاپ‌تاپ خمیر، شیشه پر پنیر، دست کی بالاست؟»** At that moment one player gently raises one hand; the middle player must guess whose hand is up. Correct guess → swap places with that player. Wrong guess → everyone chants **«رفتم در خرّازی، خرّاز گفت: سوزن می‌خواهی یا قیچی؟»** and he must choose: «سوزن» (needle) → players poke his back lightly with fingertips; «قیچی» (scissors) → children move two fingers like scissors gently on his back. Then the game repeats.
- Players: 3+ (picture: mum + 3 children). Equipment: none.
- Camera: no (touch, guessing). Note: playful touching on the back — keep "gentle" emphasised.

- **Partner needed:** L12 play page (both games are 2-player); all family games; L7 star (grown-up measures); L12 story asks the child to interview a parent.
